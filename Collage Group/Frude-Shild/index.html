Project Abstract & Summary

FraudShield is a two-in-one platform combining cyber fraud detection with public education, developed under the Problem Based Learning (PBL-II) curriculum at Silver Oak University, Department of Information Technology[cite: 6, 7].

In Gujarat, cities like Ahmedabad, Surat, and Rajkot have experienced a sharp rise in digital financial frauds (over 11,000 cases in 2025 and 200+ daily complaints to Helpline 1930)[cite: 2, 3, 7]. FraudShield enables citizens to verify suspicious phone numbers/links, view city-wise heatmaps, receive real-time scam alerts, and engage with Gujarati/Hindi educational content to contribute toward the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047[cite: 6, 7].

Primary Purpose

Prevent citizens from falling victim to online financial fraud through early threat detection, real-time alert feeds, and accessible regional awareness tools[cite: 6, 7].

Key Problem

Traditional awareness (posters/pamphlets) lacks interactive engagement[cite: 1]. Vulnerable groups like senior citizens and small business owners are repeatedly exploited via urgency-driven calls[cite: 3, 5].

Academic Affiliation

Aditya Silver Oak Institute of Technology, Silver Oak University, Ahmedabad (Academic Year 2024-2025)[cite: 6, 7].

Integrated Research Studies (Gujarat Region)

Summaries of five specialized research papers underpinning FraudShield's domain logic[cite: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5]:

Research Paper 1 • NCRB Trend Analysis[cite: 2]

Rise in Digital Financial Frauds in Gujarat (2021-2024): An Analysis of NCRB Trends Post-COVID-19

Abstract: Analyzes the post-pandemic surge in financial cybercrime across Ahmedabad, Surat, and Rajkot[cite: 2]. Highlights how rapid post-COVID UPI adoption led to increased vulnerability to fake KYC and phishing, with Helpline 1930 receiving 200+ daily complaints[cite: 2].
Research Paper 2 • Scam Patterns[cite: 3]

Analyzing Scam Patterns in Ahmedabad: Urgent Calls and SMS Messages in Financial Fraud

Abstract: Investigates tactics reported by the Cyber Crime Cell of Ahmedabad, focusing on manipulation of urgency (urgent KYC suspensions, instant fake loans, phishing URLs) that exploit victim fear[cite: 3].
Research Paper 3 • Helpline Data[cite: 4]

High Volume of Online Financial Fraud Complaints in Gujarat: Helpline 1930 Analysis

Abstract: Evaluates over 73,000 complaints recorded in 2023 alone across urban and rural Gujarat, identifying significant underreporting, investigation backlogs, and the need for region-specific tools[cite: 4].
Research Paper 4 • Demographics[cite: 5]

Digital Illiteracy and Fraud: Victimization among Elderly Citizens & Small Business Owners

Abstract: Probes why 70%+ of elderly citizens and small shopkeepers in Tier-2/3 Gujarat fail to distinguish genuine SMS from phishing links, highlighting emotional stress and retirement fund losses[cite: 5].
Research Paper 5 • Interactive Tools[cite: 1]

From Posters to Protection: Need for Interactive Fraud Awareness Tools in Gujarat

Abstract: Demonstrates the failure of static pamphlets/posters and outlines the FraudShield framework: gamified quizzes, regional Gujarati videos, real-time alerts, and scam verification[cite: 1].

Hardware & Software Requirements

Frontend

HTML5, CSS3 Glassmorphism, JavaScript ES6, GSAP Animation Engine, Lucide Vector Icons[cite: 6, 7].

Mapping API

Leaflet.js & OpenStreetMap tile engine for plotting spatial heatmap markers across Gujarat cities[cite: 6, 7].

Data Architecture

ScamDB, Reports Queue, Heatmaps Schema, User Roles, and Chatbot Query Logs[cite: 6, 7].

Hardware Specs

Dual Core Processor, 2GB RAM Minimum, 4G/Wi-Fi connection, Mobile & Desktop Responsive[cite: 6].
